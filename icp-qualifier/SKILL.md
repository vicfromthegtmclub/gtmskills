## What it does

Give it a company name or domain and your ICP definition. It returns a score,
the three signals that drove the score, and a one-line reason to pass or work
the account.

## Setup

Edit `references/icp.md` with your own criteria before you install it. The
scoring is only as good as the definition you feed it.
