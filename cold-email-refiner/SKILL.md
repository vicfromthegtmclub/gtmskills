## What it does

Paste a cold email. The skill scores it against 14 rules pulled from campaigns
that clear an 8.5% reply rate, tells you which ones fail, and rewrites the
failing parts instead of handing back a vague critique.

## When to use it

- Before a sequence goes live
- When reply rate drops and you cannot tell why
- Reviewing copy written by someone else on the team

## How to install

1. Download the zip below
2. In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills
3. Upload the zip and enable it

## Rules it checks

- Subject line under 4 words
- No paragraph over 2 lines
- One ask, at the end, and only one
- Zero feature mentions before the pain is named
